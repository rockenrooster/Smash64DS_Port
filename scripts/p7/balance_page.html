Smash64DS Balance Sheet

Every fighter's attacks as the game runs them: hitbox damage, launch angle, knockback, size and the frames each hit is out. Pick a fighter, or compare one move across the whole roster.

Frames are animation frames at 60 per second, counted from 1; "end" means the hit stays out until the move ends. Startup is the first frame a hit is out.
Angle 361 is the Sakurai angle. KBG knockback growth, BKB base knockback, Fixed set knockback (fixed knockback replaces growth when it is not 0). Size is the hitbox radius in game units.
Throws list the throw's own launch (damage, angle, knockback) apart from any hitbox the throw animation makes. Projectiles and hits made by a fighter's code (fireballs, blasters) are not in move scripts and are not listed, except Palutena's Explosive Flame.
Inherited moves come from the fighter's base (Palutena from Peach, Ike from Roy) where no script of their own replaces them.
