Smash64DS Balance Sheet

Every fighter's attacks as the game runs them: hitbox damage, launch angle, knockback, size and the frames each hit is out. Start with the fighter scores and the outlier list, then open a fighter's sheet or compare one move across the whole roster.

Frames are animation frames at 60 per second, counted from 1; "end" means the hit stays out until the move ends. Startup is the first frame a hit is out.
Angle 361 is the Sakurai angle. KBG knockback growth, BKB base knockback, Fixed set knockback (fixed knockback replaces growth when it is not 0). Size is the hitbox radius in game units.
Throws list the throw's own launch (damage, angle, knockback) apart from any hitbox the throw animation makes. Projectiles and hits made by a fighter's code (fireballs, blasters) are not in move scripts and are not listed, except Palutena's Explosive Flame.
KB 100% is the hit's knockback on a standard-weight target at 100% once the hit lands (the game's formula, no handicap): one number for KBG, BKB and Fixed together.
Scores rank each fighter's standard moves (the compare list) against every fighter with the same move: Damage by Total %, the move's damage when every hit lands (a box out in several windows hits once in each; clean and late boxes count as the stronger; a rapid jab counts its strongest hit), Power by KB 100%, Speed by startup (faster is higher). KBG and BKB score the same hit's knockback growth and base knockback on their own (they are inside Power already, so Overall leaves them out). Each is the mean percentile, so 50 is the roster middle and 100 the top on every move; Overall is the mean of the three. Specials, projectiles and combos are not scored.
Outliers are values at least 2.5 robust deviations (median absolute deviation) from the roster median for the same move and at least 25% away from it (startup: 3 frames and 30%; KBG and BKB: 10 and 25%). red reads stronger than the roster (more damage or knockback, faster startup), blue weaker. Grabs are compared on startup only, and a move whose boxes all show 0 damage (its damage set by code) is not compared on damage or power. A far-off value can also be a script the sheet reads wrongly, so check it in game before changing it.
Inherited moves come from the fighter's base (Palutena from Peach, Ike from Roy) where no script of their own replaces them.
