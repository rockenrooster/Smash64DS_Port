## Package: ball and selection chain

**Outcome:** The real source ball pickup/throw/hit/open path selects and creates the correct summon with source credit and cleanup. Merely spawning several unopened balls in a CPU match proves none of this chain.

Use `itMainMakeMonster` and the manager/source progression guards. The source Mew branch uses a 1/151 roll subject to its unlock condition; ordinary selection excludes the relevant previous spawns per source history. Preserve branch order/random calls and actual current scene/1P bonus behavior. Do not approximate the distribution from repeated random screenshots.

**Proof:** Host source selection/eligibility/history boundary tests, then natural ball open→summon for the relevant creator path. A controlled test seed can target a legal outcome, but final proof still reaches the normal opener/maker; direct maker injection alone is not acceptance.

## Package: complete native summon batches

Batch source-equivalent generator shapes; for every kind cover body/material/animation roots, sibling/child roots, child weapons/items/particles, source cry and lifetime. Position/orientation/depth/alpha must be correct over spawn/attack/exit. Large body or particle output is still required even when the atlas is full.

MLucky requires actual Egg/container behavior; no damage-only substitute. Pippi's dispatched families require their complete visual/behavior closure, not only a Clefairy body. Qualify source-dispatched variants with table/host coverage and natural representative engagement without creating a new per-kind proof-mode fleet.

## Package: concurrency, attribution and retirement

Determine legal simultaneous source ball/summon/child counts from source pools/selection and scene configuration. Measure that admitted scene set; do not invent a smaller limit. Child damage belongs to the correct source player/team even after the ball despawns or another item is picked up. Test source owner KO, target loss, hit/miss and scene exit. Required resources outlive every real consumer.
