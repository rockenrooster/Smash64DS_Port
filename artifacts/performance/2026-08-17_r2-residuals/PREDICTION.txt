WHAT WOULD FALSIFY IT, AND WILL BE REPORTED LOUDLY IF SEEN
  - c254 two-VBlank materially ABOVE c250 (say >= 1,975, i.e. R1-at-HEAD better
    than R2-at-HEAD by more than the cross-HEAD gap being explained). That would
    make the -4 a real cost of the switch and would be a finding about the
    switch, not about placement. It will be reported as such and NOT explained
    away.

  - A presented count far off 2,043, which would mean the two arms are not the
    same fight and no comparison is licensed at all.

NOTE ON WHAT THIS ARM CANNOT DO
  Even a perfect null does not prove the switch is free. It proves R1 and R2 at
  the SAME HEAD land in the same place on THIS histogram, under THIS both-CPU
  stress arm, over ONE run each. c250 and c254 still differ in link placement
  from each other (different BUILD dirs, different object layout is possible),
  so "within noise" is the only conclusion available; an exact tie would be
  luck, not proof.
