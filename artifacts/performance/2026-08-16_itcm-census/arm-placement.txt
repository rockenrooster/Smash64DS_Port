WHAT THIS TABLE SETTLES.  The float BODIES this campaign replaced are ALL in
.main, exactly like the fixed bodies that replaced them.  The one ITCM-resident
member of any pair is ndsRendererMtxMul20p12 -- the FIXED arm of the 5.14x
prior.  So the 5.14x is the one comparison with a placement asymmetry and it
runs TOWARD fixed point, not away from it.

The real asymmetry is one level down: a float body in .main executes its
arithmetic inside ITCM-resident library leaves (__aeabi_fmul/fadd/fdiv,
__ieee754_sqrtf, all ITCM above) and pays `bl` overhead instead of fetch, while
a fixed body inlines that arithmetic into its own .main bytes, which ARE
fetch-charged.  That trade has been measured twice, both times against fixed:
CAMERA_Q20_12.md section 6 (+3,032 B of inlined leaves turned -4,736 into
+1,600) and the collision ring's K-ICACHE null.
