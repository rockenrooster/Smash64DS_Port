Rows 6 (+ probably 5): WHY effect textures are refused. The reason mask is
read, and it is a RESOURCE BOUND, not a malformed request.

ANSWER: reason = 0x400 = bit 10 = NDS_RENDERER_HW_TEXREJECT_ALLOC
(nds_renderer.c:1423). ndsRendererHardwareAllocTexture() returned NULL. The
first-rejection census says why:

    free=0  live=48  pinned=28  evictable=0  thisframe=20

The hardware texture cache is 48 entries (NDS_RENDERER_HW_TEXTURE_CACHE_COUNT,
:1470). 28 are PINNED and 20 were already TOUCHED THIS FRAME. 28 + 20 = 48.
Nothing is free and nothing MAY be evicted, so the next texture request in that
frame is refused. This is SLOT exhaustion, not VRAM byte exhaustion -- the
distinction the census exists to make.

Cycle 69, 2026-08-04. Candidate: builds/build-c69-rejectreason (the ordinary
proof ROM plus NDS_R2_STAGE_ROUTE_PROBE=1, which is the only thing that compiles
the reason-mask writer).

======================================================================
WHY THIS PRODUCES "DIM AND UNTEXTURED TOGETHER"
======================================================================
Proven by source read, and it is why the two symptoms are one event:

  ndsRendererHardwareRejectTexture(..., ALLOC)      :14712 (alloc site)
    -> ndsRendererHardwareResolveOrBindTexture returns FALSE
    -> use_texture = FALSE                          :16711
    -> state->texture_prepare_enabled = FALSE       :16719
    -> the polygon is DRAWN WITH TEXTURING DISABLED, not skipped

An object that loses this race still draws -- it draws its flat material colour
with no texture modulation. That reads as dark AND untextured, on the same
object, in the same frame. The owner's "same objects, dim and untextured
together" is one defect, not two.

======================================================================
WHY EVERY REJECT COUNTER READ ZERO -- A RETRACTION
======================================================================
Cycle 68 reported "no upload ever fails; every reject counter zero". THAT WAS
WRONG, and the error is worth recording because the counters looked clean.

ndsRendererHardwareRejectTexture (:12757) records its fence UNCONDITIONALLY at
:12761, but only bumps a counter inside `if (stats != NULL)` at :12763. The two
published counters read by the probe -- gNdsFighterDLAllDrawHardwareTexture
RejectCount and gNdsEffectRendererTextureRejectCount -- are per-owner views of
stats->hardware_texture_reject_count. The rejecting path here is neither of
those owners, so it rejected 2,592 times while both counters sat at 0.

The fence is the counter that cannot lie, because it is upstream of the guard.

======================================================================
WHAT THIS SETTLES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
======================================================================
SETTLED:
- The mechanism is slot exhaustion at the texture cache, refusing a bind, and
  the refused polygon drawing flat. Chain link that breaks: REQUESTED ->
  REJECTED, upstream of glBindTexture and upstream of poly format. Both of
  those links are exonerated.
- It PREDATES the cycle-68 mode-3 fix and that fix did not change it: the c66
  and c68 ROMs are bit-identical on every cache counter at all eight tags,
  including fencefirstframe=355.
- Row 6 is ORTHOGONAL to row 9. The 24 pinned stage textures never drop
  (cycle-68 audit). They are not the victim here -- they are 24 of the 28
  pinned slots, i.e. a major cause of the pressure.

NOT SETTLED, and deliberately not assumed:
- Whether Fox's reflector (down-B, BUGS row 5 "green, should be blue") is the
  SAME defect. The hypothesis is strong -- a refused texture draws flat material
  colour, and "green" would then be the fallback colour rather than a palette
  bug -- but it needs the REFLECTOR'S OWN reject fence, not an inference from
  the halo's. It also needs the routing question answered first: the reflector
  may be on the PARTICLE ATLAS path (ndsRendererHardwarePrepareIFCommonAtlas,
  :10269, which allocates outside this cache and is a different upload site) or
  on the cached DL path traced here. Only the latter is this bug. The atlas
  moved A5I3 -> A3I5 on 2026-08-03 specifically because of the reflector, so it
  has history on the atlas path.

======================================================================
VRAM HEADROOM -- THE QUESTION THAT DECIDES THE FIX. THERE IS ROOM.
======================================================================
Texture VRAM allocation, nds_platform.c:330-336, the NDS_RENDERER_HW_TRIANGLES
path this ROM takes:

    vramSetBankA(VRAM_A_TEXTURE)          128 KB  texture
    vramSetBankB(VRAM_B_TEXTURE)          128 KB  texture
    vramSetBankC(VRAM_C_MAIN_BG_...)              main BG (2D overlay)
    vramSetBankD(VRAM_D_MAIN_BG_...)              main BG
    vramSetBankE(VRAM_E_MAIN_SPRITE)              OAM sprites
    vramSetBankF/G(VRAM_x_TEX_PALETTE_SLOT0/1)    texture PALETTE, not texels
    vramSetBankH(VRAM_H_SUB_BG)                   sub screen

  texture VRAM total      262,144 bytes (A + B)
  battle static set        61,696 bytes (staticspan; 0x6800000-0x680F100)
  remaining               200,448 bytes  = 76.5% FREE

THE STRONGER PROOF, which does not depend on that arithmetic: bit 12
NDS_RENDERER_HW_TEXREJECT_TEXIMAGE (:1425) NEVER FIRES. The mask is 0x400 and
only 0x400, at every tag, across 2,592 rejections. TEXIMAGE is what a refused
glTexImage2D sets -- the DS texture allocator declining to place texels. It
declined ZERO times in a whole match. The renderer ran out of cache ENTRIES
while texture VRAM still had roughly three quarters of itself free.

REGIME: raising NDS_RENDERER_HW_TEXTURE_CACHE_COUNT is the lever that matches
the measurement. It will NOT simply move the rejection from bit 10 to bit 12,
because bit 12 has never been anywhere near firing.

Also note the static set does not even reach one bank: :11350 picks a 1-bank or
2-bank mask on (preparedBytes > VRAM_B - VRAM_A), and 61,696 < 131,072, so it
takes one. Bank B is texture-configured and largely unused by the static set.

======================================================================
COST ESTIMATE -- FLAGGED AS AN ESTIMATE, NOT MEASURED
======================================================================
NDSRendererHardwareTextureCacheEntry (:3427) is 13 u32/int fields (52 bytes),
plus u32 key_hash when NDS_RENDERER_PROFILE_LEVEL < 2 (this ROM: level 0, so
+4), plus NDSRendererHardwareTextureKey. The key is described in-tree as a
"236-byte DS cache footprint" (:14212), giving roughly 292 bytes per entry.

    48 entries (today)   ~14.0 KB main RAM
    96 entries           ~28.0 KB   (+14.0 KB)
   128 entries           ~37.4 KB   (+23.4 KB)

CONFIRM BEFORE QUOTING: print sizeof(NDSRendererHardwareTextureCacheEntry) or
add a _Static_assert rather than trusting the 236 figure second-hand.

Watch NDS_RENDERER_HW_TEXTURE_LOOKUP_COUNT (128u, :1471) -- it is the hash
lookup table for the cache and is only 2.7x the current entry count. Raising
entries toward 128 without raising the lookup table changes its load factor.

PERFORMANCE, NOT ONLY CORRECTNESS: with evictable=0 the surviving entries churn
-- re-convert and re-upload every frame, permanently, which is the owner's "it
also affects FPS for the rest of the match". gNdsRendererProfileTextureConvertTicks
(:14638) is already instrumented and is the before number for that A/B. This
makes the fix gate-relevant, so it needs a proper matched A/B on a fresh budget,
NOT a tired one. Not started here on purpose.

======================================================================
THIS IS A DECISION, NOT A COSMETIC FIX
======================================================================
Per BUG_FIXING_PROCESS.md ("When the gap is a decision, stop iterating"), a
resource bound cannot be closed by iteration. The bound is 48 cache slots with
28 pinned. Options, costs NOT yet measured, and NO recommendation chosen here:

  a. Raise NDS_RENDERER_HW_TEXTURE_CACHE_COUNT above 48. Costs RAM per entry
     (NDSRendererHardwareTextureCacheEntry carries a ~236-byte key, :3427).
  b. Shrink the pinned set. 28 of 48 slots -- 58% of the cache -- are pinned,
     and 24 of those are the battle static texture set.
  c. Permit eviction of touched-this-frame entries under pressure. Cheapest in
     RAM, but risks re-uploading a texture already used this frame, which is
     both a cost and a flicker risk.

Sacrifice order and RAM budget make this the owner's call.
