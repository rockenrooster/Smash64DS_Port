FPS drops a lot, the scene gets dimmer and some things lose textures."

HEADLINE: in three full one-minute matches -- one on the pre-fix ROM and two on

1. THE PINS -- the primary result
======================================================================
gNdsRendererBattleStaticTexturePreparedCount is a LIVE count, not a high-water
mark: zeroed at prepare (nds_renderer.c:11018), incremented once per prepared
record (:11321), and zeroed again on teardown (:11383). It is additionally
checked fail-closed against the expected key count at :11354. So 24 means 24 are
resident right now, and a drop would read as a smaller number or 0.

BEFORE the halo: 24. DURING: 24. 1,007 frames AFTER the maker fired: 24. The

The pinned-HIT counter is the independent confirmation and it does not depend on

textures cannot serve hits. The pins are alive and working when the owner's
report says they should be gone.

This also positively confirms the row 9 fix (commit 4e18101cca) is holding: the

======================================================================
Zero at EVERY tag in ALL THREE runs:

  gNdsFighterDLAllDrawHardwareTextureRejectCount   (freject)     0
  gNdsEffectRendererTextureRejectCount             (ereject)     0
  gNdsRendererParticleAtlasFailCount               (atlasfail)   0
  gNdsRendererBattleStaticTextureViolationCount    (staticviol)  0
  gNdsRendererStageOwnerAbortCount                 (abort)       0
  gNdsRendererBattleStaticTexturePrepareFailCount                0

ndsRendererHardwareRejectTexture (:12757) bumps
stats->hardware_texture_reject_count UNCONDITIONALLY at :12765, and that is what
feeds the two published reject counters. Both read 0, so there were no
rejections to attribute -- which makes the ALLOC / GENTEX / TEXIMAGE split moot
for these runs. Neither "cache thrashing" nor "VRAM exhausted" is happening.

CAVEAT, so nobody reads a false zero later: the reason mask
gNdsRendererProfileTextureRejectReasonMask IS present in the c68 ELF, but its
writer is behind NDS_RENDERER_PROFILE_LEVEL >= 2 or NDS_R2_STAGE_ROUTE_PROBE
(:12767-12777). Reading it on this ROM would be the counter-with-no-compiled-
writer trap. It needs a flag-enabled build to mean anything, and it is not
needed while the unconditional reject counters are 0.

3. PRIM DOES NOT ANIMATE -- two independent confirmations
======================================================================
(a) ASSET. A scan of all 13,616 bytes of 85.vpk0.bin finds exactly ONE
    G_SETPRIMCOLOR in the entire file:

      FFFFFFFF  x1  at 0x28a8

    and exactly ONE combine decoding to mode 3, at 0x28a0 -- eight bytes
    earlier, in the same list. The prim write and the mode-3 selection are
    adjacent commands. There is no second prim value anywhere in the file to
    animate to. Distinct prim values presented at the mode-3 bake = 1.

(b) THE A/B ITSELF. The pre-fix ROM does not take the mode-3 branch at all, so
    prim is not in its key; the post-fix ROM keys on prim. If prim varied, the
    two would diverge on cache behaviour. They are BIT-IDENTICAL at all eight
    tags, on every counter:

      memofill    9  9  9  12  12  15  15  18   (both ROMs)
      memostale   0  0  0   3   3   6   6   9   (both ROMs)
      pinnedHits  1748 5396 6954 8431 14897 20983 44237 68984 (both ROMs)
      ownerreject    1    1    1   40   168   298   335   501 (both ROMs)

    18 cache fills across a whole match. A per-frame variant storm over the
    halo's ~200-frame life would be in the hundreds.

The coordinator independently killed this from the source while this was being
measured: dEFManagerRebirthHaloEffectDesc (efmanager.c:1648) declares MatAnimJoint
offset 0x0 -- no material animation joint, so an animated prim has nowhere to
live -- and efManagerRebirthHaloMakeEffect (:5994) writes no colour at all.
Three independent lines agree. The variant storm does not exist and c68 did not
introduce one. Keying on prim is correct and costs exactly one cache entry.

======================================================================
4. THE LIGHTING CANDIDATE -- strongly narrowed, not killed
======================================================================
sNdsR2EpochUnlitVertexColor (:19224) is written at ONE site, :19489, inside
ndsRendererNativeShadeProductionActions (:19430, 2 callers). Every read is in
the native FIGHTER production path:

  :20431  ndsRendererNativePrepareProductionRun        (drops POLY_FORMAT_LIGHT0)
  :19608  ndsRendererNativeShadeProductionActions      (skips diffuse/ambient)
  :20607  ndsRendererNativeEmitProductionRawTexturedRun
  :20648  ndsRendererNativeEmitProductionRawUntexturedRun
  :20707  ndsRendererNativeEmitProductionPrimitiveGroups
  :20776  ndsRendererNativeEmitProductionCrossRun

and the colour it substitutes is ndsRendererR2DenseVertexColor15(dense_id),
which reads sNdsNativeFighterDenseVertices (:19230).

THE STRUCTURAL POINT: the revival halo is an EFFECT, drawn through the effect DL
path (the adapter's ndsRendererExecuteDisplayListWithVertexCache). It never
enters ndsRendererNativeShadeProductionActions, so it CANNOT write this flag.
The stage is not drawn through these emitters either, so it cannot read it.

Therefore a stale flag can only mis-light FIGHTER geometry, using a value left
by another FIGHTER epoch. That is exactly what E32's dark-maroon hurt flash was,
and it is a real staleness risk worth closing -- but it is NOT a mechanism for a
scene-wide dim, and the revival platform cannot be its trigger. It is not the
leading explanation for the owner's wording.

TO KILL IT PROPERLY needs one counter pair -- writes at :19489 versus reads at
:20431 per frame. nm finds NO such instrumentation in the c68 ELF (no
UnlitVertex / EpochUnlit / ShadeCallCount / ShadeFrameCount symbols), so this is
the one question here that genuinely requires a build. Not built; awaiting the
orchestrator's call.

======================================================================
5. THE DISCRIMINATOR CANNOT BE APPLIED, AND THAT IS THE REAL GAP
======================================================================
"Does the dimmed geometry still have its texture" separates one bug from two.
It cannot be answered from these runs because NO capture shows dimming OR
texture loss. All seven captures per run are clean, and the two ROMs differ by
1,126 px in one 52x22 box (the beam) against a same-ROM floor of 0.

So the honest state is: THE SYMPTOM DID NOT REPRODUCE. Three things are worth
checking before hunting a mechanism, in cost order:

  a. WHICH ROM the owner watched. These runs are the lab
     smash64ds-battle-playable-proof-hwtri, not the published
     smash64ds-battle-playable-hwtri. Rows 6 and 9 were reported from normal
     play. The flags differ.
  b. THE FPS HALF MAY BE THE HARNESS, NOT THE ROM. probe-ko-vfx.ps1 stops the
     core at eight capture points and runs a PowerShell screenshot helper while
     stopped; the window freezes each time. One run this cycle (c68run1) stalled
     long enough in that helper that the guest advanced ~50 frames and every
     later event-armed tag drifted. Somebody watching that window would see
     "FPS drops a lot" with nothing wrong in the ROM at all. This does NOT
     explain dimming or texture loss.
  c. A LONGER OR DIFFERENTLY-DRIVEN MATCH. These runs force two early KOs at
     fixed frames and finish in one minute. The owner plays.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP, not taken: get the owner to say which ROM and roughly
when, or capture a run on the published ROM under ordinary play. Hunting a
mechanism for a symptom that no counter in three runs registers is the expensive
order.
