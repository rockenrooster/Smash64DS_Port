--- Per-kind arena charge at ftManagerSetupFilesMainKind ------------------------
This is the currency P2-2's byte law is written in: one allocation per distinct
kind, deduplicated against what is already resident.

  Mario    54,048   (NDS_P2_BASE_FIGHTER_ALLOC_SIZE_ROWS 0xcb)
  Luigi    41,552   (57,104 standalone, less 15,552 shared with Mario)
  Donkey   77,360   (79,648 standalone, less the 2,288 MiscData pair)
  CAPTAIN 100,160   (102,448 standalone @ 0xec, less the same 2,288 pair)
  Fox     116,752   (119,040 standalone, less 2,288)

The 2,288 B is MiscData201 (2,096) + MiscData299 (192), which every landed kind
already makes resident -- the same deduction Fox and Donkey take.

       margin 24,356 B over the 25,600 B floor.
  Measured argmax over landed content  Fox+Captain+Donkey+Luigi = 335,824 B
    -> +46,112 B, roughly twice the whole margin, and that is BEFORE the
       20,064 B this row already spent on targets that build him.

  This row does not close that. Two things follow and are stated rather than
  fixed:
   * the stress arm's roster is no longer the argmax PROJECT_GOAL.md asks for,
     and re-pointing it needs a byte lever first, not a roster edit;
   * a user can reach four distinct kinds from the shipped character select
     (four slots, five kinds), and nobody has measured a four-distinct-kind
     match driven from the SHELL at any roster, roster 2 included.

  Nearest measured lever, still untaken (P2-3r13): the two graphics-heap
  contexts hold 96 B of a 106,496 B reservation, overflow 0, over a whole match.
