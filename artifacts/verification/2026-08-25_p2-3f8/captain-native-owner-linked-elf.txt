  sNdsNativeCaptainRootLightPreambles is 0x20 = 4 entries of 2 u32 -- the UNION
  of the high and low preamble sets, which is the point: the low model's root 6
  carries 0xffffff00 / 0x804c3300 and the high model never emits it. The two
  Tables structs are `b` (bss) because NDS_NATIVE_OWNER_IMAGE_CAPTAIN=1 fills
  them from the NitroFS image at fighter creation.

  CrossPaletteSlots is 17 bytes of 0x1f (PACKED_GX_SLOT_CURRENT) in BOTH
  details: Falcon claims no physical palette slot at all.
